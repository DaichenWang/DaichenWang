I want to start with the moment this project really began. On the ninth of February this year I was standing in the waiting hall of Wuxi Railway Station. It was the busiest week of the Lunar New Year. Everyone around me was going home. In a few days I would be flying back to Brisbane. That gap is where the project sits. For some people, going home is just part of the year. For others it turns into a trip you save up for, and then one you stop planning at all. There are tens of millions of Chinese people living overseas, and for many of them the wish to reconnect with where they came from stays strong long after going back stops being easy.
