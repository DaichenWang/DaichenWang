I want to start with the moment this research actually began, because it explains the whole thing better than an abstract can. On the ninth of February this year I was standing in the waiting hall of Wuxi Railway Station. It was the peak of the Lunar New Year rush. Everyone around me was going home, and in a few days I would be flying back to Brisbane. That is the tension the project sits inside. For some people return is simply a rhythm of life. For others it has become a periodic exception, something you plan and save for and eventually stop planning. The Chinese diaspora numbers in the tens of millions, and for a great many of them the desire to reconnect with where they came from stays powerful and persistent long after returning stops being practical.
