Which brings me to what has not been built. Despite a lot of very good XR heritage research, there is no peer-reviewed, empirically validated social VR environment designed specifically for the cultural and spatial needs of the Chinese diaspora. The tools people already use — WeChat, video calls, social media — do sustain relationships across distance, and I want to be fair to them. What they cannot do is reproduce the embodied spatial familiarity Fei identified as the bedrock of that soil-bound world. You can see your mother's face on a screen; you cannot walk through the courtyard behind her. On the research side, the preliminary VR-for-diaspora studies either lacked cultural specificity or narrowed to student populations, and none of them centres the classical Chinese garden as a carrier for root-seeking.
