So two platforms were built, and they are one designed environment rather than two projects. On the left, the high-fidelity world: Unity 2022.3 through VRChat for Meta Quest 3, ninety frames per second in a three by three metre volume, with a desktop fallback so nobody is excluded for lack of a headset. On the right, Minecraft Java Edition on a custom server with an AI cultural guide through MinePal — configured rather than engineered: I wrote it a character brief covering classical poetry, Daoist naturalism and garden design, rather than training anything. The line at the top is the important one. Minecraft is the methodological engine of Phase Two, not a demo. Its coarse blocks keep building visible and reversible for people with no 3D modelling skills, and its message boards and personal courtyards let them contribute when they are not in the room.
