Where it stands today. Data collection is complete across all three phases. The VR environment is fully operational, holding ninety frames per second on Quest 3, and it has been publicly exhibited. The Minecraft platform with the MinePal guide is complete, with over ninety per cent conversational accuracy across roughly twenty test rounds. A hundred and two artifacts scanned from Jichang Garden — using handheld iPhone LiDAR, Gaussian Splatting and aerial photogrammetry — are openly available on Sketchfab. The one thing still in progress is the thematic analysis of the June focus-group data, and a third iteration in Unreal Engine.
