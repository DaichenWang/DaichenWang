The work makes three contributions, and they are addressed to three different rooms. To diaspora and memory studies, a theoretical one: a model of root-seeking as sensory and embodied memory activation, which is what happens when you put Fei's Xiangtu in dialogue with Brah's homing desire. To digital heritage and human-computer interaction, an empirical one: to my knowledge the first peer-reviewed, empirically evaluated social VR environment tailored to Chinese diaspora cultural needs. And to community-centred design practice, a methodological one: a co-design model for culturally specific XR that other people can run. The line underneath matters for how you should read all of this. The empirical findings are situated in this case. The theoretical model and the methodology are the parts designed to travel.
