The study answers those in three phases. Phase One was survey work and building. A global online survey of the Chinese diaspora returned two hundred and six questionnaires across two rounds, of which a hundred and seventy-two were retained. On site at Jichang Garden I worked with fifty visitors and staff, twenty-seven of whom completed the questionnaire — fifteen visitors and twelve administrators — and demonstrations reached forty people. Phase Two took the prototype to Brisbane: twelve participants in two groups of six, in June. I recruited through Chinese-language social media, the Chinese Students and Scholars Association, and deliberately through a local table tennis club, because I did not want a room full of students. Phase Three was folded into those same sessions: pre- and post-experience state questionnaires plus a twenty-six item experience measure covering interface, immersion, cultural recognition and social presence. All of it under QUT ethics approval.
