The doctoral inquiry sits at two levels. The overarching question is bigger than any single platform: when permanent physical return is no longer viable, how can the sensory, spatial and social conditions of cultural belonging be reconstituted elsewhere? I operationalise that through one designed case, and I want to be precise about the word case. The garden is an instrumental case, not the object of the thesis. Underneath it sit three subsidiary questions. Which cultural, spatial and sensory elements do diaspora members themselves identify as essential. How participatory co-design can genuinely engage a diaspora community in shaping something culturally authentic. And how participants actually experience the environment in terms of belonging and emotional reconnection.
